A calmer way to support everyday wellbeing.

Holistic Loader brings several practical wellness tools together in one welcoming place. Instead of asking you to follow a complicated program, each experience begins with a clear need: sleep, stress, energy, nutrition or mindful play. Choose what matters now and begin immediately.

Breathing and sound for moments of stress

Guided breathing can provide a simple rhythm when the day feels busy. Pair it with the Soundscape Creator to combine calming sounds and shape an atmosphere for rest, focus or a quiet break.

Gentle movement and better daily habits

Yoga Loader offers an approachable path into movement, while the Nutrition Tracker helps you notice patterns and build consistency. The goal is not perfection. The goal is a useful next step that feels manageable today.

Mindful games with a distinctive atmosphere

Intuition Loader, Mahjong Soundbath and the growing game collection add discovery and play to the platform. They are designed as inviting experiences that fit the same visual world as the wellness tools.

Use Holistic Loader on a phone or desktop whenever you want a brief reset. Start with one tool, return to the experiences that work for you, and create a personal routine at your own pace.

Questions

Good to know

What is Holistic Loader?

Holistic Loader is a browser-based collection of guided breathing, soundscape, yoga, nutrition and mindful game experiences.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Holistic Loader runs in your browser on phone and desktop.

Where should I start?

Choose the goal that best matches how you want to feel today. Each goal card takes you directly to a suitable tool.

Is Holistic Loader free?

Yes. Holistic Loader is free to use thanks to the generosity of donors who help keep it running.
